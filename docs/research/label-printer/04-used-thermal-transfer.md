## Recommendation
Zebra GK420t, ceiling A$250 delivered with power supply and USB cable. Fallback: used TLP2844 at A$130 or less delivered.

## Gotchas
1. LP2844 and GK420d are direct thermal; buy only TLP2844 or "t" models.
2. reboot-it says "direct thermal and thermal transfer"; ask them to confirm ribbon spindle and platen are fitted. Warranty wording conflicts (3 vs 12 months).
3. Overseas eBay listings carry high shipping and no AU warranty.

## Used-unit checklist
- Seller prints a config label (hold feed on power-up): odometer, firmware, mode.
- Platen roller: no flat spots or glaze.
- Full-width bar test print: no missing dots.
- Power supply, USB cable, ribbon spindles included; lid latches; ribbon sensor present.
